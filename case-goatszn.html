The brief

Most streetwear stores look loud but sell soft — generic templates, weak product pages, no reason to add a second item. GoatSZN needed to feel like a premium editorial magazine and behave like a high-converting store at the same time.

The audience is young, mobile-first, and impulse-driven, but price-sensitive — so the design had to build desire and remove every reason to hesitate at checkout.

The approach

A warm, cream editorial system: oversized serif display, disciplined grid, generous whitespace — the opposite of the cluttered marketplace look. Restraint reads as premium.

Then, underneath the calm surface, I engineered the product page as a conversion surface: bundling, tiered savings, sizing confidence, and social proof, each earning its place.

What the store had to do

The conversion engine

The centrepiece is a "Frequently bought together" bundle with tiered discounts — Buy 2 for 15% off, Buy 3+ for 20% off. It reframes the decision from "should I buy this tee?" to "which set should I grab?"

Live savings math (₹5,997 → ₹3,897) makes the value undeniable, and a single "Claim offer" adds the whole curated set in one tap — lifting average order value without a discount race to the bottom.

Details that remove doubt

Size confidence guidance kills the #1 fashion return reason before it happens. Verified-buyer reviews, a clear delivery timeline, 7-day exchange, and secure prepaid via Razorpay each close a specific gap in the shopper's confidence.

Nothing is decorative — every block on the page answers an objection or nudges the cart.

Beyond the storefront

The admin panel behind it

A store that sells well creates a second problem: what do you make next, and where is the ad money actually working? Shopify answers neither well — its reporting stops where the brand's real decisions start.

So I built GoatSZN a custom admin panel: one place where production and ad spend are decided from the store's own numbers instead of instinct.

What to manufacture

Sell-through by design, colourway and size — so the next production run follows real demand instead of a guess, and the sizes that actually sell are the ones that get made.

Ad performance & ROAS

Spend against revenue per campaign, broken out day by day — so a weekday that quietly burns budget shows up as that weekday, not buried inside a monthly average.

Best & slow movers

Which drops earn their shelf space and which quietly tie up capital — ranked, so retiring a design is a decision with a number behind it.

Orders & fulfilment

Order flow end to end, with the prepaid-versus-COD split visible — the number that decides how much working capital a drop really needs.

Stock alerts

Low stock and size stockouts surface before they cost a sale, instead of being noticed when a customer can't find their size.

Bundle & AOV tracking

How often the tiered bundle actually fires and what it moves average order value to — so the conversion engine on the product page is measured, not assumed.

The storefront sells. The panel decides what to sell next.

Outcome

GoatSZN ships as a store that reads like a brand and sells like a machine: an editorial front that earns attention, and product pages where bundling, tiered savings, sizing confidence and social proof compound into a higher average order value.

And it doesn't stop at the sale — the admin panel turns what the store learns back into decisions: what to manufacture, what to retire, and which day of ad spend actually earned its money.

It's the clearest proof of what I bring to any client — interfaces that are beautiful and built to move the metric that matters.
