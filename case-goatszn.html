The brief

Most streetwear stores look loud but sell soft — generic templates, weak product pages, no reason to add a second item. GoatSZN needed to feel like a premium editorial magazine and behave like a high-converting store at the same time.

The audience is young, mobile-first, and impulse-driven, but price-sensitive — so the design had to build desire and remove every reason to hesitate at checkout.

The approach

A warm, cream editorial system: oversized serif display, disciplined grid, generous whitespace — the opposite of the cluttered marketplace look. Restraint reads as premium.

Then, underneath the calm surface, I engineered the product page as a conversion surface: bundling, tiered savings, sizing confidence, and social proof, each earning its place.

The goals

What the store had to do

  • Look premium in three seconds — earn trust before the first scroll.
  • Turn one purchase into a set — lift average order value without racing discounts to the bottom.
  • Remove every checkout hesitation — sizing doubt, trust, delivery, payment.
  • Stay fast & mobile-first — the audience shops on their phones, on impulse.

The conversion engine

The centrepiece is a "Frequently bought together" bundle with tiered discounts — Buy 2 for 15% off, Buy 3+ for 20% off. It reframes the decision from "should I buy this tee?" to "which set should I grab?"

Live savings math (₹5,997 → ₹3,897) makes the value undeniable, and a single "Claim offer" adds the whole curated set in one tap — lifting average order value without a discount race to the bottom.

Details that remove doubt

Size confidence guidance kills the #1 fashion return reason before it happens. Verified-buyer reviews, a clear delivery timeline, 7-day exchange, and secure prepaid via Razorpay each close a specific gap in the shopper's confidence.

Nothing is decorative — every block on the page answers an objection or nudges the cart.
